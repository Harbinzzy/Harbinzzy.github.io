Abstract

Image dehazing has witnessed significant advancements with the development of deep learning models. However, most existing methods focus solely on single-modal RGB features, neglecting the inherent correlation between scene depth and haze distribution. Even methods that leverage depth estimation to assist dehazing often suffer from suboptimal performance due to insufficient exploitation of reliable depth information.

We present UDPNet, a general framework that leverages depth-based priors from the large-scale pretrained depth estimation model DepthAnything V2 to boost existing image dehazing models. The Depth-Guided Attention Module (DGAM) adaptively modulates features via lightweight depth-guided channel attention, while the Depth Prior Fusion Module (DPFM) enables hierarchical fusion of multi-scale depth features through dual sliding-window multi-head cross-attention. These components integrate depth priors into the restoration network and help it adapt to varying haze densities, illumination conditions, and domain gaps across synthetic and real-world data.

Extensive experiments demonstrate improvements across daytime, nighttime, real-world, and remote-sensing dehazing. UDPNet improves FSNet by 0.85 dB on SOTS-Indoor, 1.19 dB on Haze4K, and 1.79 dB on NHR, and also extends to five-task all-in-one image restoration.

Depth-guided Restoration

From relative depth to adaptive attention and multi-scale feature fusion.

01

Extract structural priors

A frozen DepthAnything V2 model estimates relative scene depth from the hazy image, providing complementary geometry cues.

02

Adapt with DGAM

Depth-guided channel attention adaptively modulates features to account for the relation between scene structure and haze.

03

Fuse with DPFM

Hierarchical cross-attention fuses depth and RGB features at multiple scales before the decoder reconstructs a clear image.

Understanding the Depth Prior

Reliable geometry cues, adaptive fusion.

UDPNet uses depth as an adaptive structural cue. DGAM and DPFM learn how to combine it with RGB features rather than directly imposing the estimated depth on the reconstruction.

Depth estimator and inference cost

The depth estimator is frozen but remains part of inference. On Haze4K, the FSNet restoration network grows from 13.28M to 13.60M parameters; the external DepthAnything V2 model adds its own parameters and computation. The following end-to-end measurements use an RTX 3080 Ti.
