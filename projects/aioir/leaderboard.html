READING THE RESULTS

Compare with context.

01

Source-reported results

Baseline values come from the survey’s Tables II–VI (arXiv v3). New results link directly to original paper tables. These are reported results, not independent re-evaluations.

02

Settings stay separate

WeatherStream, Real-World and WeatherBench have independent rankings. Training mixtures, image channels and model variants may differ across papers. Consult the row details before drawing a strict comparison.

03

Averages are traceable

We preserve each paper’s reported average. DCMPC-Net and WeatherRemover use an explicitly computed mean over the three All-Weather test sets. PSNR and SSIM are higher-is-better; LPIPS is lower-is-better.

Data updates & corrections +

The legacy survey names the denoising set BSD68; some recent papers name it CBSD68. Missing parameters are left unreported. Read the source audit and protocol notes ↗
